# Tools

The stdio wrapper exposes these 15 tools by default. All of them are annotated read-only, non-destructive and
idempotent. Extra keys are refused (`additionalProperties: false`).

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `calculate_french_income_tax` | French income tax 2026 (Article 197 CGI, family quotient) |
| `calculate_french_salary` | French gross to net salary 2026 (cadre / non-cadre / civil servant) |
| `calculate_us_federal_tax` | US federal income tax 2026 (IRS brackets + standard deduction) |
| `calculate_uk_income_tax` | UK income tax 2025/26 (HMRC bands, allowance taper) |
| `calculate_canada_federal_tax` | Canadian federal income tax (CRA brackets + basic personal amount) |
| `calculate_mortgage` | Monthly payment, total interest, optional amortization schedule |
| `calculate_compound_interest` | Lump-sum compound growth with yearly breakdown |
| `calculate_loan_payment` | Generic amortizing loan (term in months) |
| `calculate_percentage` | Percent of total / percent change / what-percent |
| `calculate_vat_generic` | Add or remove VAT/GST/sales tax at any rate |
| `calculate_bmi` | Body Mass Index + WHO category |
| `calculate_tdee` | Maintenance calories from BMR + activity level |
| `list_bundles` | Discover the 31 thematic bundles of the full catalog |
| `get_bundle_tools` | List the tools inside one bundle |
| `call_any_calculator` | Invoke any of the 446 hosted calculators by name |

## Parameters

### calculate_french_income_tax

- `income` (number, required, min 0): annual net taxable income in euros.
- `parts` (number, default 1, 0.5 to 20): number of fiscal shares (1 = single, 2 = married, +0.5 per child).

### calculate_french_salary

- `gross_monthly` (number, required, min 0): gross monthly salary in euros.
- `status` (default `cadre`): `cadre`, `non_cadre` or `fonction_publique`.

### calculate_us_federal_tax

- `income` (number, required, min 0): gross annual income in USD.
- `filing_status` (default `single`): `single`, `married_joint`, `married_separate` or `head_of_household`.

Federal only: state income tax and FICA are not included.

### calculate_uk_income_tax

- `income` (number, required, min 0): annual gross income in GBP.

National Insurance is not included and Scottish bands are not modelled.

### calculate_canada_federal_tax

- `income_cad` (number, required, min 0): annual income in Canadian dollars.

Federal only: provincial/territorial tax and CPP/EI are not included.

### calculate_mortgage

- `principal` (number, required, min 1): loan amount in currency units.
- `annual_rate` (number, required, 0.01 to 30): annual interest rate in %.
- `years` (number, required, 1 to 50): loan duration in years.
- `with_schedule` (boolean, default false): include first 12 months + last month amortization.

Insurance, taxes and fees are not included.

### calculate_compound_interest

- `principal` (number, required, min 0): initial amount.
- `annual_rate` (number, required): annual interest rate in %.
- `years` (number, required, min 0): investment duration in years.
- `compounds_per_year` (number, default 12, min 1): compounding frequency per year.

Formula: A = P(1+r/n)^(nt). For loans, use `calculate_loan_payment` or `calculate_mortgage`.

### calculate_loan_payment

- `principal` (number, required, min 1): loan amount.
- `annual_rate` (number, required, 0.01 to 50): annual interest rate in %.
- `months` (number, required, 1 to 600): loan duration in months.

### calculate_percentage

- `operation` (required): `of` (X% of Y), `change` (from A to B) or `what_pct` (X is what % of Y).
- `a` (number, required): first value.
- `b` (number, required): second value.

### calculate_vat_generic

- `amount` (number, required, min 0): amount.
- `rate` (number, required, 0 to 100): tax rate in %.
- `mode` (default `ht`): `ht` (before tax) or `ttc` (after tax).

### calculate_bmi

- `weight_kg` (number, required, min 1): weight in kilograms.
- `height_cm` (number, required, min 50): height in centimeters.

Screening indicator only, not a medical diagnosis; not valid for children or athletes with high muscle mass.

### calculate_tdee

- `bmr` (number, required, min 1): basal metabolic rate in kcal.
- `activity_level` (required): `sedentary`, `light`, `moderate`, `active` or `very_active`.

### list_bundles

No meaningful parameter (an optional unused `_unused` string is accepted). Lists the 31 thematic bundles of the full
catalog. Use it first when a calculator is not among the core tools.

### get_bundle_tools

- `bundle_id` (required): one of `finance-france`, `finance-belgique`, `finance-suisse`, `finance-us`, `finance-uk`,
  `finance-afrique-quebec`, `finance-universal`, `immobilier`, `sante`, `sport`, `math`, `conversions`, `education`,
  `construction`, `science`, `auto-transport`, `vie-quotidienne`, `temps-rh`, `cuisine`, `animaux`,
  `astronomie-nature`, `voyage`, `jardinage`, `photographie`, `musique`, `crypto`, `jeux-probabilites`, `plomberie`,
  `textile-mode`, `fun`, `energie`.

Returns the tool names and descriptions inside the bundle.

### call_any_calculator

- `tool_name` (string, required): exact name of the target calculator as returned by `get_bundle_tools`
  (for example `calculate_french_notary_fees`).
- `arguments` (object): arguments of the target calculator. Pass `{}` if it takes none.

The call is forwarded verbatim to the hosted macalc API (network request, no authentication). It returns the target
calculator's JSON result (`result`, `formula`, `source`, `reference_url`) or an error if the tool name is unknown.

## Workflow for the full catalog

1. `list_bundles` to find the right domain.
2. `get_bundle_tools` with the `bundle_id` to get the exact tool name and its parameters.
3. `call_any_calculator` with that `tool_name` and its `arguments`.

Results are estimates and not tax, medical or financial advice. More on the
[MCP info page](https://macalculatriceenligne.com/mcp/) and in the
[API docs](https://macalculatriceenligne.com/api-docs/).
